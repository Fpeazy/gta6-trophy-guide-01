    Missable — 6 trophies to do while the moment is live

    Each of these depends on a single moment or a state that is hard to recreate. Do them when their guide says, before the moment passes — the other 49 trophies stay open.

    • Hot Together — lose a 4-star wanted level with your partner beside you; do it during a partner story beat.
    • Retail Therapy — rob 3 shops back-to-back with your partner without losing the wanted level.
    • Catch Me If You Can — make an unscheduled departure at VCIA; do it the first time you have a plane at the airport.
    • Sneakthief — rob a jewelry store without being detected; one detection fails the attempt.
    • Capital Offense — smash your way out of a vehicle showroom; a one-time moment.
    • Let’s Go Bowling — follow through at the Kolt & Associates bowling alley, while the alley is open to you in the story.

    Roadmap to the Platinum

    Built from the official descriptions: story chapters and missions first, then contacts and strangers, then the counted grinds, then activities and combat, then economy and cleanup. One stage at a time — the platinum lands with the last trophy.
